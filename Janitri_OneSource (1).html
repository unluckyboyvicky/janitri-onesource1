About Janitri

Janitri Innovations Private Limited is an Indian medical technology company dedicated to saving lives at birth. Founded in 2016 by Arun Agarwal, we build smart, wireless and clinically validated monitoring solutions for mothers, fetuses and newborns — covering the entire 1000-day journey from the first trimester through a child's second year of life.


Our mission: "To see a world where no mother and baby dies during pregnancy, delivery and post-delivery."

Travel Policy
Domestic Travel
Economy class for flights above 3 hours. Train (AC 2-tier) for shorter distances. Pre-approval required for all trips.
International Travel
CEO pre-approval mandatory. Economy class by default. Business class for flights above 8 hours with MD approval.
Accommodation
Budget hotel for domestic. Standard business hotel for international. Actual cost reimbursed with receipts.
Expense Claims
Submit within 7 days of return with original receipts. Use the expense form on the HR portal.
Daily Allowance
Per diem applicable for domestic trips beyond 1 day. International per diem as per destination. Refer to policy doc.
Local Conveyance
Cab receipts reimbursed for official travel. Personal vehicle: ₹8/km for two-wheeler, ₹12/km for four-wheeler.
Code of Conduct

At Janitri, we hold ourselves to the highest standards of integrity, respect and professionalism. Every team member is expected to act honestly, treat colleagues and customers with dignity, protect confidential information, avoid conflicts of interest and represent Janitri's mission with pride.


Key principles: Act with integrity in all dealings. Treat every colleague, customer and partner with respect and dignity. Protect all patient, company and investor data. Declare and avoid any conflict of interest. Never misrepresent Janitri's products, capabilities or financial position. Violations of this code may result in disciplinary action including termination. For the full Code of Conduct document please contact hr@janitri.in.

POSH Policy

Janitri has a zero-tolerance policy for sexual harassment in any form. We are fully compliant with The Sexual Harassment of Women at Workplace (Prevention, Prohibition and Redressal) Act, 2013. An Internal Complaints Committee (ICC) is constituted and operational for confidential complaint resolution.


If you have experienced or witnessed any form of harassment please contact posh@janitri.in immediately and confidentially. All complaints are treated with complete confidentiality and responded to within the statutory timeframe of 90 days. You will not face any retaliation for raising a complaint in good faith.

Data Security
Patient Data
All patient data is encrypted at rest and in transit. Never share patient information outside the Janitri platform.
Device Security
Lock your laptop when stepping away. Use company-approved devices only for official work.
Password Management
Use strong, unique passwords for all work accounts. Enable two-factor authentication wherever available.
Network Safety
Avoid using public Wi-Fi for work. Use the company VPN when working remotely or from a public location.
Data Storage
Store work files on company-approved cloud platforms only. Do not use personal drives for company data.
Report Incidents
Any suspected breach or security incident must be reported to it@janitri.in immediately. Do not delay.

Frequently Asked Questions
How do I apply for leave?
Log into the HR portal and submit a leave request. Select the correct leave type and provide a reason. Your manager will be notified for approval. Planned leave requires at least 3 working days notice. For emergency leave, inform your manager directly and apply on the portal at the earliest.
What is the expense reimbursement process?
Submit your expense claim within 7 days of the expense using the claim form on the HR portal. Attach all original receipts. Claims are processed within 15 working days of manager and finance team approval. For large claims above ₹50,000, additional approval from your business head is required.
How do I raise an IT or equipment issue?
Contact the IT helpdesk at it@janitri.in or raise a ticket on the internal support portal. For urgent issues during working hours, call the IT support number. For laptop replacements or new equipment, get your manager's approval and raise a request via HR portal.
How do I escalate an HR concern?
You can raise a concern directly with your manager, your skip-level manager or the HR team at hr@janitri.in. For POSH or sensitive matters, contact the Internal Complaints Committee at posh@janitri.in directly and confidentially. For unresolved concerns, you may escalate to the CEO at arun@janitri.in.
What health insurance does Janitri provide?
Janitri provides group health insurance covering the employee and immediate family (spouse and up to 2 children). The policy includes cashless hospitalisation at network hospitals across India. The insurance card and policy document are shared on joining. For any claims support contact hr@janitri.in.
How do I access my payslip?
Payslips are available on the HR portal under My Documents. Salary is credited on the last working day of each month. For any payroll discrepancies contact finance@janitri.in with your details.
What is the notice period at Janitri?
The notice period is 30 days for employees in their first year and 60 days thereafter, unless otherwise specified in your appointment letter. Notice buyout may be considered at management discretion. Please refer to your employment contract for exact terms.
